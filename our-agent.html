Our Agent

The WPS AI Agent

A purpose-built system, scored on the same benchmark as the general-purpose models on the leaderboard, as a reference point for what a system built specifically for WPS analysis can achieve.

Try the live demo ↗

What it's built to fix

General-purpose AI models tend to fail the same way on WPS content: they perform well when a prompt spells out the gender dimensions, and lose most of that performance on the terse, realistic requests practitioners actually send. Where they do respond, they often state claims with more confidence than the evidence supports, and rarely flag what they don't know. The WPS AI Agent is designed directly against those two failure modes — weak WPS reasoning without explicit cues, and unverified, overconfident claims.

How the answer is checked

Checking is the piece that most directly answers the "unverified claims" failure mode. Code checks that every figure and citation in the answer traces to the passage it came from, and a second model reads the answer against those passages. The Sources list is built by code from the citations the answer uses. Since v2 introduced verification, factual integrity and acknowledgment of uncertainty have gone from the agent's weakest scores to among its strongest, ahead of every general-purpose model tested. The agent does not use internet search.

Where it stands today

Version 4 of the agent scores above every model on the leaderboard. On GPT-5.6 Sol it scores 0.87, leads every general-purpose model tested on all eleven criteria, and clears the pass mark on all eleven. The margins are widest on the two failure modes this agent was built against. On factual integrity it scores 0.91 against 0.56 for Claude Opus 5, the strongest general-purpose model both overall and on this measure; on gender-sensitivity and WPS integration, 0.89 against 0.58. The live demo runs v4 on GPT-6 Luna, which scores 0.86 and answers in under a minute; it leads every general-purpose model on ten of the eleven criteria, and trails Qwen3.8 by 0.005 on WPS guardrails, where most systems score near the ceiling.

Against v2 on GPT-5.6 Sol, v4's largest gains are trust building (+0.14), due diligence (+0.09) and policy alignment (+0.08). Trust building is still v4's lowest score, at 0.75, and analytical depth, at 0.78, has the most room to improve. The full breakdown for both builds is under Benchmark results below.

This is a demonstration of what purpose-built scaffolding can add to WPS analysis. Results should always be read alongside a human WPS advisor, not in place of one.

What the structure is worth, and on which base model

GPT-5.6 Sol is the one base model tested all three ways: on its own, inside v2 and inside v4. That makes it the clearest view of what the agent's structure adds.

Base model Bare v2 v4 What the structure adds
GPT-4o 0.334 0.712 — v2: +0.378
GPT-5.6 Sol 0.601 0.839 0.873 v2: +0.238 · v4: +0.272
GPT-6.1 Sol 0.603 — 0.868 v4: +0.265
GPT-6 Luna not tested — 0.860 —

On its own, GPT-5.6 Sol clears the pass mark on fewer than half the criteria, failing in the ways the Benchmark describes. Inside v2, it clears the pass mark on every criterion. Verification lifted factual integrity and acknowledgment of limitations from its weakest scores to well above the pass mark, and the gap between cued and sparse prompts closed: it brings a gender lens to terse requests as reliably as to detailed ones.

v4 keeps those gains and adds to them, mostly in trust building, due diligence and policy alignment. Those are the parts of an answer that say who should act, what could go wrong and which commitments apply. Overall, the model goes from 0.60 on its own to 0.84 inside v2 and 0.87 inside v4.

The other base models fit the same pattern. GPT-6.1 Sol, a newer model, scores no better than GPT-5.6 Sol on its own, and v4 lifts it by about as much. v2 lifted the older GPT-4o even further, far enough to outscore the best Sol model tested on its own, GPT-6.1 Sol. GPT-6 Luna, which runs the demo, hasn't been tested on its own.

July 2026
WPS Agent v2, GPT-5.6 Sol base
v2's workflow, unchanged, moved from GPT-4o to the newer GPT-5.6 Sol. Every criterion improved, and the gap between cued and uncued prompts, the pattern this benchmark exists to detect, disappeared.
0.84
July 2026
WPS Agent v2, GPT-4o base
Rebuilt v1 as a four-stage workflow: intake, domain specialists, synthesis and mandatory verification. It kept v1's base model, so the gain over v1 comes from the workflow alone. It ran the live demo until November 2026.
0.71
April 2026
WPS Agent v1, GPT-4o base
The first version. It routed each request to research and policy specialists and had no verification stage, so factual integrity and acknowledgment of uncertainty were its weakest scores.
0.59
