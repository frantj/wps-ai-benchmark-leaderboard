How AI systems handle Women, Peace and Security

See how leading AI models compare on an expert-validated Women, Peace and Security benchmark. For why WPS matters to AI systems and how the benchmark is built, see The Benchmark.

AI is no substitute for a human WPS advisor or for direct consultation with women on the ground, and should not be used for unsupervised warfare decision-making. This benchmark measures relative performance among AI systems — it is not a fitness-for-deployment certification.
